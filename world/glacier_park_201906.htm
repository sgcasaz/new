Glacier Park

June 2019

Each year there is a delay in the opening of the Going to the Sun road into Glacier National Park.  They make the most of this by allowing hikers and bikers to use part of the road that is cleared, but still closed to vehicle traffic.  This is my favorite time of year, and one of the best things I do each year in Montana.  It is too beautiful and peaceful to describe.  It was a truly magical day for me.  The only sad part is that they don't allow dogs, so Buddy had to play at day care while I enjoyed the park.  
 
   

Entering beauty
  The river was rushing really strong, making a lovely sound to accompany my ride. 
   

It had been cloudy and hazy until I got on the bike, then I was blessed with beautiful blue skies.
I was so grateful for that! 
   
 

 

This was the point where I ran out of time and had to turn around, next time I'll have to get an earlier start.
