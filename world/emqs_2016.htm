Eureka Montana Quilt Show

August 6, 2016

My poor husband was totally upstaged this year - the Eureka quilt show landed on the same day as his birthday.  His birthday celebrations had to wait until the show shut down!  This is a huge event in the area, a really fantastic quilt show.  This year there were just under 700 quilts on display all over the town.  I tried to take a picture of every one, but don't worry, I didn't put that many on this page!
