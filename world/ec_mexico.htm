Eric in Mexico

April 10 - 16, 2002

Eric was sent to Hildago (near Monterey) in Mexico for a field trip.  Afterwards, the crazy nut had them drop him off at the climbing area 30 miles away.  He didn't speak the language, have a place to stay or a ride to the airport, but naturally he made friends and ended up having a wonderful time.  He did his first multi-pitch climb there. (Translation for non-climbers:  really, really high up!)
 

 

 
   
  This thing is really tall, like 500 feet, and Eric climbed to the top of one of them and stood on the very peak.  I told you he was crazy!
