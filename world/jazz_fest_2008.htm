Jazz Fest 2008

April 24 - 26, 2008

My friends Amy & Julio came over from the U.K. for Jazz Fest this year.  Laura Faulkenberry came over from Houston and I went down for a short but great weekend.  New Orleans was looking great and I had one of the best Jazz Fest days ever in my 9 years of attending.  The weather was great, the music was smooth, and the friends were fun.  it was a great time.
 
   

 My niece had sent me Flat Stanley right before I left on this trip so I was very happy to bring him along.
