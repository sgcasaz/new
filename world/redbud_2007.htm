Redbud Classic

April 15, 2007

Eric's company, GHK, sponsors everyone to participate in the Redbud Classic each year.  There are many different events, but the most popular are the 10K, 5K, and 2 mile 'Walk and Baby Stroller Derby.'  GHK buys everyone a nice dinner at Bellini's afterward and gives gifts as well - it's quite a deal!
