Kayak Koncert

Wednesday, August 23, 2006

Jeff Patterson, of the Oklahoma City Outdoor Network fame, had a great idea to do a kayak outing in which we paddled half-way across Lake Hefner to gather below Pearl's Lakeside restaurant when they were having live music on the patio.  The artist that night was Camille Harp, who was very good.  Tang Nguyen and Jeff Patterson also put together a great video of the event.
 
   
   
   

 They were having a sailing race (I think it's called a Regatta) and did
 not seem to be too happy that they had to steer around us kayaks.
 		  
   
                
 Friends Tang & Jonni cheered us on from shore.
