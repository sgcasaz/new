South Padre visit to Dan & Rose

February 2017

Dan & Rose spend some time near South Padre Island, TX each winter in order to escape some of the Montana snows.  Eric and I like to join them there when we can. It is always a fun and relaxing time for everyone.  
 
   
 The view from the deck of their house.  The dolphins often play out in the channel there. Pelicans offering to help the next-door neighbor clean his fish.
   
 Taking an evening walk on the fishing wharf, standing near the tourist pirate ship. Our visit to the beach started with Rose's chair dumping her on the sand.  She was OK! 
   
Enjoying each others' company All smiles on the beach
   
Rose & Eric  Eric, getting tired of the camera already.
   
Casazza men, happy to have the sand between their toes - and ready for a nap in the sun!  Eric is digging deep for his fake smile now.
   
I'm not much for napping, so I toured the Port Isabel light house. The view from the top.
   

A fun afternoon was spent at the Los Fresnos rodeo.  Good times! 
The spiral staircase seemed endless at the time.
