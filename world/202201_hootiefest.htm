Hootiefest!

January 26 - 30, 2022


Amy & I were blessed to attend Hootiefest in Riviera Maya Mexico in late January.  I had a scare about being able to go when I tested positive on my pre-travel Covid-19 test. Thankfully, after showing proof that I had just recovered from it two weeks earlier, I was allowed to proceed with the vacation as planned.  It was a truly great time!  
 
   

Leaving Montana, ready for some music and sunshine!
