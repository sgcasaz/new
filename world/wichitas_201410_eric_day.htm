Wichita Mountains for Eric Day

October 26, 2014

Poor Eric is a rock climber without a partner most of the time.  The situation got so desperate that he was willing to let me belay for him!  Actually, it is usually me who doesn't want to spend a whole day at the rock, but I wanted to bless this wonderful husband of mine.  After church this lovely Sunday we headed off to the Wichita Mountains to allow Eric to do a little climbing.  
 
   
 It is a pretty long hike to Pear & Apple You can see the formation now, but we still have a ways to go
   
We have arrived!  He's ready to go. Sunset on the hike out
   
Proof that I belayed my heart out - blisters!
