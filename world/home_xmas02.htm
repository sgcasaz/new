Christmas in Oklahoma

December 23 - 28, 2002

We left beautiful, sunny, 70-degree temps in Houston to travel into snow back home in Oklahoma.  Snow is no fun in Oklahoma!  We don't know how to drive on it so it's very unsafe.  Luckily we made it from the airport in Tulsa to Mom & Dad's house safely, and it melted off the roads by Christmas Day. 

We had a nice visit with my family and exchanged lots of neat gifts.  We also enjoyed a big dinner on Christmas Day, thanks to my Mom and her good cooking.
