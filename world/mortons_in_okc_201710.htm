Mortons in OKC

October 2017

My mother, Marcella Morton, was awarded the AARP Indian Elder award this year.  We were all so proud of her!  My parents and sister came to town for the award ceremony, which took place two days after we returned from Montana.  It was so nice seeing them again and celebrating my gifted mother.
