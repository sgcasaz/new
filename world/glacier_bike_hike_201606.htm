Glacier bike & hike with Amy

Friday, June 3, 2016

One typical Thursday I got an offer I couldn't refuse from my sister-in-law Amy.  She invited me to join her the following day for visit to Glacier Park, so I immediately put in a vacation request.  What a spectacular day we had.  I don't think I've ever said the word, "Wow" so many times in my life.  The weather, the views, the activity, the company - it was all just perfect.  Great day!  
 
   
 My pictures can't capture how crisp and clear the mountains looked that day Starting out on our hike to Avalanche Lake
   

 Waterfalls viewed along the hike

 
   

It was overcast while we were at the lake, but you can still see how beautiful it is there.
   
   
   These little chipmunks were bold, and fast! 
   
   
   

We were amazed by how these trees cling to almost bare rocks.
 
 

After our hike, Amy provided us with mountain bikes for the next adventure.  
The main road in the park (Going to the Sun road) was closed to cars from this point forward.
That made it such a peaceful, special place for a relaxing bike ride in incredible scenery.

 "Wow!" 
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
 This day was a very special memory for me!
