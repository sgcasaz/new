Climbing Afternoon at Point of Rocks

Wednesday, September 28, 2016

On our last week in Montana, we were blessed to go along as assistants on a rock climbing trip for some area high school boys.  It was a fun time for everyone, and a beautiful day to be outside in such a lovely place.  We went to the Stillwater Creek area of the Point of Rocks climbing area near Eureka.  
 
   
 Eric, closely supervising the boys as they gear up  It seemed like we should be whistling, "Hi ho, hi ho..."
