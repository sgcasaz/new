Summer in Montana

August 6 - 13, 2011

This year we were accompanied by the Clouse family on our yearly trip to Montana.  Seeing everything through their eyes added extra fun to the trip, especially watching the kids enjoy the ranch.  It was wonderful to see everyone and to be up there in all the beauty.  It was also a very welcome break from the brutal summer heat. It was beautiful up there!
