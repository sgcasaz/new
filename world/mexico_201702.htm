Mexico

February 12 - 18, 2017

Our super-kind neighbor Dick invited Eric and I to go with him to his time share in Mexico in the Riviera Maya area. Over time, the trip morphed into me & my Mom going on our own.  It was a very beautiful place, a perfect time of year, and a fun time for both of us.  We made lots of memories! 
 
   
The resort was huge, I think they said there were over 2,000 employees at this resort alone. The living room part of our suite.  There was also a dining area, kitchen and two bedrooms.
