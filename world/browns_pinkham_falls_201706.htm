Pinkham Falls with the Browns

June 2017

Eric & I were unsuccessful at finding Pinkham Falls when we tried on our own in May, so I asked some local experts to take me there.  My friends the Browns gave me a great escort to the area.  The rivers were running high at this time of year so the falls were very impressive.
