Hong Kong

July 1997



John Castelli and I went to Hong Kong and mainland China for about 10 days on Phillips business. We were in Hong Kong about two weeks after the British/Chinese handover.

We saw a few anti-China demonstrations, but for the most part things seemed very calm (thankfully.)

We spent two days in Hong Kong, then 5 in China, then 3 in Hong Kong. It was a good trip!
