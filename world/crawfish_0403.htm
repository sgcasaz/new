WesternGeco Crawfish Boil 2003

Each year WG puts on an awesome crawfish boil.  This year was no exception, we had a great time!  It was even more fun because we took our Norwegian friend Ingvar with us.  This was his first crawfish boil and we had quite a time showing him the ropes.
