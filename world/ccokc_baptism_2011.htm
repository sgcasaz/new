CCOKC Baptism

Saturday, June 18, 2011

It was another great year for baptisms at Calvary Chapel of OKC.  Each summer we have a cookout and baptism at Lake Arcadia.  Yes, it's my favorite church at my favorite lake celebrating my favorite thing - Jesus!  Does it get any better than this?  Well, if you could have knocked about 15 degrees off of the stifling heat that would have been nice, but hey, I was just glad it wasn't storming or something.
