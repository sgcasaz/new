West Kootenai with Dan & Rose

June 26, 2015

Dan & Rose are having a log cabin built for them by an Amish man who lives in the West Kootenai.  Each Friday night some of the Amish people over there put on a big dinner for the public like a 'one night only' restaurant.  We enjoyed the beautiful drive over there, a lovely meal at the Amish, and a tour of the first floor of the cabin in progress.  Nice!
 
   
 The first floor of the cabin was complete  The logs are huge!
