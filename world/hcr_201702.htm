Horseshoe Canyon Ranch climbing trip

February 2017

Eric joined friends Kraig, Rusty, and Cort on a climbing trip in Arkansas to one of their favorite spots, Horseshoe Canyon Ranch.  Along with a great weekend of climbing and camaraderie, Eric was also honored to spread some of our dear friend Luc's ashes. HCR had been a very special place to us when Luc & Cassy lived in Texas.  Lots of fun memories here!
