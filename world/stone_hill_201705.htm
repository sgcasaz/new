Stone Hill Climbing

Saturday, May 13, 2017

Our friends Brad and Jennifer are great people to know, as they are always instigating fun activities in the area.  On this day they decided to host a climbing party at Stone Hill.  We had a camp fire, tons of food, friendly chipmunks stealing food, and lots of rock climbing.  It was a good day!
