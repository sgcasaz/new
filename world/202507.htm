One of our major projects in July was seeing the new roof finished on The Timbers Lodge.

This roof was about 30 years overdue.  We're so proud to have protected this wonderful old building with a great quality roof.  It's beautiful! 

This month also marked the start of our new relationship with Hope Church Eureka.  They've renovated the basement and have
taken over running the Timbers Event Center.  It's also now their church home.  It's a great partnership!
