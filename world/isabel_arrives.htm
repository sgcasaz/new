Baby Isabel Li arrives!

Tuesday, February 26, 2013

Our good friends Wei & Jiayi were blessed with their first baby this month, and what a sweetheart she is.  I was blessed to get to meet her the day after she was born and I took these pictures of the little cutie.  Jiayi encouraged me to hold her (because I was afraid to, she was so small!)  Mom and baby are both healthy and happy, thank you Jesus! 

Here are the details everyone always wants to know: She was born around 10pm after 5 hours of labor. She weighed 6 pounds, 11 ounces and is 21 inches long.
 
   
When I arrived she was expressing herself about the 
general discomfort of a wet diaper.		 Now Momma has changed her and things are starting to
improve, although she is sticking her tongue out at me! 
   
 I almost got a smile out of her there. Jiayi is able to get her to smile though, isn't she cute?! 
   
 Here I am, bravely holding her - she fell right to sleep!
