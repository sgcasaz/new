Music Builds tour

October 5, 2008

I LOVE the band "Jars of Clay."  They are one of my absolute favorite bands.  The iTunes Original version of their song, "Frail" is my favorite song.  I was very excited when I heard they were coming to town.  I had a really great night at this concert.
 
   
 Here you can see how great my seat was. Jars of Clay greeted fans and signed autographs afterward.

and considerate guy that it has made me more of a fan of this band.
