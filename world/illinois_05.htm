Our Trip to Illinois - Day 5

October 13, 2002

  Page 1  |  Page 2  |  Page 3  |  Page 4 
We took our time getting up on Sunday (surprise, surprise) and then headed for St. Louis.  We got there in time to spend an hour or so at the arch.  Unfortunately, it also happened to be the hour leading up to the St. Louis / Oakland game which took place right next to the arch.  It was crowded with insane fans everywhere!  The arch was nice though and we had a smooth trip home.
