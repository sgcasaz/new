94. Summer Breeze 

Unfortunately, this quilt has a LONG story.  While I was quilting it, I was dragging the machine to
the edge in order to change the bobbin when I accidentally pressed the "needle down" button.  The
machine obeyed as I was moving it fast and ripped through the front, batting and back - all three.  

  

I tried everything I could think of to fix it.  This was the best I could do, 
shown at the bottom of this picture below.
   
   

It fit so nicely on a queen bed, but no one wanted a quilt with a darned place on it.  

Years later I reworked this quilt, cutting off the two sides to make a Twin quilt out of it.
Then I used the sides that I'd cut off to make a baby quilt.  Both of those sold quickly!  
Here is the patch from the front.
