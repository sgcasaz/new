Amy's Birthday Float

July 21 - 23, 2016

My good friend and sister-in-law Amy had a landmark birthday this summer.  Her ever-thoughtful husband Jeb decided to plan a surprise camping/float trip for her to celebrate.  They were kind enough to include me on this trip and I had a great time with them and their friends.
