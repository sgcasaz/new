Yuta and Mai Visit Montana

May 18 - 22, 2017

We met our Japanese friend Yuta at the ELS center in OKC in the spring of 2017 and immediately hit it off with him.  He shared our love of the outdoors and had a great spirit for adventure.  His wife Mai joined him in OKC later and as we began our move to Montana, they decided to visit us there.  We had many great adventures together exploring beautiful Montana together.
