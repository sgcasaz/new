Visit to the Hefner's Ranch

Saturday, September 6

OU Football is a huge thing in these parts, so to get us initiated into the the right attitude, the Hefners were nice enough to have us over for a watch party on Saturday.  OU played Alabama and won, so everybody was happy!  The Hefners have a very nice ranch up near Guthrie.  They have lots and lots of interesting and exotic animals.  I only took pictures of a few but we got to see a whole bunch.
