Ritchie's Going-Away Pub Crawl

December 14, 2001

 
Our good friend Ritchie Wayland is being transferred back his homeland in the UK.  To give him a proper send-off, we all met for a pub crawl in Rice Village which started at Two Rows, went to the Village Ice House, and finished at the Ginger Man when they closed their doors at 2am.  Needless to say, Eric and I didn't make it that long!!
