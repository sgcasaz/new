112. Interlocked 

This quilt can be yours, it's still for sale in my Etsy shop! :-) 

  

I bought the floral print fabric on sale at a Kalispell quilt shop.  I'm sure the lady was just 
making conversation as she took my money, but she asked, "What are you going to do
with this fabric?"  I didn't want to answer, "Put it in the closet next to all the other fabric." 

So basically, she shamed me into making something out of it right away.  I thought it was
a pretty quilt, but it just hasn't found the right person yet.

 123. Native Bison

Another purchase by our sweet friend Nigel for his home in Scotland.  

  

I am super proud of this quilt because I designed the pattern myself.  I did take
the giant feather idea from another quilt pattern, "Be As a Feather" by Gingiber.

I also got the inspiration for the arrows from "Made by Marzipan" but I made 
mine differently.  Anyway, it was very popular and I ended up making several like it. 
  It won a ribbon in the Rendezvous Days 2023 show. 
   
  I don't remember what possessed me to do the back like this.
