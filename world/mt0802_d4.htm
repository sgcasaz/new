Most of the brothers had to leave on Sunday morning.  School was starting that week plus Dan & Tracie are in the process of moving.  We took it easy for most of the day, then visited with Lucas & Kristi for awhile.  Later we all went to John & Karen Leonard's new house.  They were trying to unpack boxes and move in, but they accepted our visit very well.  We had a really nice time catching up with them.
