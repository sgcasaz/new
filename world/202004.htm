April Adventures

April 2020


 
We started the month of April on the road, making an early move from Oklahoma to Montana.  Because of Covid-19, the oil and gas industry was slow, so Eric was anxious to be somewhere that he could stay active.  We had no idea at the time that this would be our last family migration, as we have since sold our OKC home and are now permanent residents of Montana.  Big changes!
