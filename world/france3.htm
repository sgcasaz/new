Paris  -  Chamonix  -  Notre Dame  -  Eric's


On my last day in France, Eric and the gang went off to work.  I had a few hours to spend before I had to leave for the airport, so Cheryl Blackerby went with me to tour Notre Dame.  It was great, so beautiful and impressive.  I took so many pictures, you'll feel like you were right there with me!
