Lunar Eclipse

September 2015

There was a lunar eclipse on Sunday, September 27.  I go to bed early so I only stayed up late enough to catch the first half of it.  I'm told it turned red as it came back out of shadow.  That would have been neat to see, but a good night of sleep is pretty neat too!
