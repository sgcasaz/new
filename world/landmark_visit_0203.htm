Visit from our Landmark Friends

Tuesday, February 18

What a blast from the past!  We hadn't seen Larry Fink or Alison Begeman in almost 4 years, so it was quite a treat when they came to town for a conference and made time to get together with everyone.  Of course, we were happy to see Peter Laudon and Rob Jefferson too!  Everyone had a great time catching up and telling stories about the 'old days.'  Thanks for organizing it Alison!
