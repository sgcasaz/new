Eric's Trip to Guatemala

April 2004

Eric's parents (Dan & Rose), besides being ranchers in Montana, are also Missionaries.  They have done most of their work in Guatemala.  Eric went along on their latest trip which was for the grand opening of Dan's biggest project yet.  It is a shelter for the children who live in the Guatemala City dump.  It gives them a place to spend the non-school part of their day, to get cleaned up and fed a warm meal, and most importantly to keep them out of the gangs.  It was a very ambitious project that came out perfectly - which goes to show that it was truly blessed by God.
