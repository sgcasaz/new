Celebration at the Three Wild Turkeys' Ranch

July 4, 2004

We were invited to the ranch of Charles Hefner, one of Eric's co-workers, for their annual 4th of July celebration.  It is a party on an amazing scale - there are tons of activities, people, food, and entertainment.  A great time was had by all.
