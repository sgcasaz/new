This day Eric went climbing with Mark & Jeb, I went golfing with Amy & Amber, then when Mark went to play basketball Amy & I joined the boys for lunch in town.  That night we went to the Lincoln county fair - we looked at the livestock, ate a "God burger", and went to the Bull Thing.  (If it was held down south it would be called the Bull Thang.)
