Ode to Zack

2010

We had to let our little Zackie go in May of 2010. We had him for 17 years, 12 of which he had diabetes. He did amazingly well until late 2009, when age and the disease slowly began to catch up with him. We kept him with us for as long as we felt it was humane to do so. He was such a treasure.

Zack was love—and nothing else. All he wanted was to love anyone who would pick him up and hold him. He and I slept face-to-face for many years. He was such a snuggler. I made this page so I can come back from time to time and relive some of my favorite memories of him.
 
I couldn't bear to go to the pound, so Eric brought baby Zack and Fuzz home to me. Who could possibly resist this little face?
 
I was so thrilled with my two little bundles of happiness. A blurry photo, but one that perfectly captures his adorable kitten days.
 
Sometimes the kittens would tumble end-over-end trying to make it down the stairs. A very young Lucas and Dallas entertaining Zack and Fuzz.
 
Those two were always together. They got into all kinds of adventures side by side.
 
They wrestled... a lot. And they loved posing like bookends.
 
They shared toys... ...and endured the "treat" of a trip to the park wearing harnesses and leashes together. I think I scarred them for life that day.
 
Most of all, they shared naps. Lots and lots of long naps. Zack had a white undercoat that made him difficult to photograph, but it also meant he could leave cat hair on absolutely every color of clothing.
 
Zack was our climber. Here he balances on top of a door like it was the most natural thing in the world. Fuzz was about to take over the toy whether Zack liked it or not. Zack was a lover, not a fighter. Fuzz... was definitely the fighter.
 
One of the millions of belly rubs Zack enjoyed over the years. Eric had the magic touch—I could never quite do it the way Zack wanted. There's the sweet little face I miss so much.
 
Zack often had a wonderfully blank expression on his face, while Fuzz always seemed to know exactly what was happening. He would cry loudly whenever we took him outside, even though he actually enjoyed being there.
 
On bird patrol with his brother. Notice who managed to claim the better spot closest to the window. This is exactly how we loved to sleep—our faces pressed right up against each other.
 
Around this time their sister, Prissy, joined the family. Zack quickly found himself at the bottom of the household pecking order because he was simply too gentle to stand up to the other two. The boys posing together once again.
 
This wasn't really the peaceful snuggle it appears to be—it was more of a battle of wills. I'm pretty sure Zack eventually surrendered and climbed down. These two were professional nappers.
 
Another wonderful picture of Zack's sweet face. Eric bundled him up, and of course Zack absolutely loved it.
 
Zack looking as adorable as ever during Christmas. One of our favorite anniversary photos from 2009 with our precious Zack.
 
He always loved nibbling on fresh grass. Zack letting us know it was bedtime and that we were running late.
 
We bought him this bed after Fuzz passed away. We called it his "Muffin Cup," and Zack thought it was the greatest trade ever. One of the last photos I ever took of him. He became very thin during his final months, but he was still our sweet little Zack.
 
This was Zack's first meeting with Rocket, and unfortunately their relationship never really improved after that. We simply kept them separated, with Rocket spending the days outside and the nights in the guest room. After Zack passed, Rocket—and later Feisty—helped begin a new chapter in our home.

Every cat we've ever had has brought something special into our lives, but I doubt I'll ever have another cat who captured my heart quite the way Zack did. He was the sweetest little soul I've ever known, and I still miss him every day.
