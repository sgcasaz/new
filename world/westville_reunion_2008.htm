Westville High School Class of 88 reunion

June 28, 2008

I cannot believe it has been 20 years!  There were 55 of us in our graduating class and I think about 15 made it to the 20 year reunion.  It was really a good time.  Thanks to Lance, Tonia, and Eddie for all the time, trouble, and expense they put into arranging it.  I loved it!
