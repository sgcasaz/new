Lower 48 Christmas Party

December 19, 2002

As part of Lower 48 Information Services, I was invited to the Lower 48 Christmas party.  It was held at Sam Houston Race Park and part of the entertainment was that the track was open and betting was going on.  Eric made two $20 bets - he lost the first one and made $20 on the second one.  Luckily, he called it quits on the betting after that!

It was a nice party and we saw many friends that we hadn't seen for quite awhile.
