EMQS - Eureka Montana Quilt Show

2025


A great group of ladies and I work all year long for this one day each year - EMQS.  We have over 400 quilts displayed all over the town for one day only.  It is a labor of love, but man, is there a LOT of labor involved!  This year was sad because it rained.  That made it much more difficult, less enjoyable for me, and harder work for our team.  

This is my 4th year of being chairman of the quilt show.  Before I took over, there was one year where it rained.  In my four years, it has rained on 3 of the 4 show days.  :-( 
 
   

This wonderful team of ladies accepts, measures, labels, and sorts over 400 quilts in one day.  It's amazing.

the time I took over.  We got back to doing them this year - this is my collection.

One of our best displays at the show is our Quilts for Hero's (which I think should be Heroes, but
no one asked me.)  Area ladies make patriotic quilts for the show which are then donated to the 
PTSD ward at the nearest Veteran's hospital.  We donate at least 14 quilts per year.
Both of our golf cart drivers took their meal break at the same time, so I
suddenly became the driver.  Having no experience I'm happy I didn't wreck!
