Visit from Dan & Rose

January 9 - 12, 2004

We were so happy to welcome Dan & Rose as our first house guests.  And what an easy couple they were to host!  We had a great time catching up, touring the town, eating lots, and getting in some good rest as well.  Dan & Rose came up from their winter hideout in Los Fresnos, TX.  They spend a few weeks down there every year, recharging and preparing for the end of another Montana winter.
