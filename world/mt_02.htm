Family started to arrive late Thursday night, so we spent time visiting with the new arrivals on Friday morning.  We had lunch in town with oldest brother Larry, his wife Mary Beth, and Katie, their youngest daughter.  Then it was back to the ranch to help prepare for the rehearsal dinner, which was held at the ranch and was a great success.
