My Favorite Houston

August 2003

Scenes of my favorite things in Houston that I will miss.  
 

 

 
 Central Market - the only place where you can enjoy grocery shopping 

 

   The House of Pies - never better than at 2 am!

 
 

 
 Lakewood Church - it's like a party every Sunday

 

 Not just liquor - some of the neatest things you'll ever find are in this store.  It's fun just to browse, although they prefer it when you buy!
   
  
Nilo Nikos -  Hands down the best Greek food and the best owners ever

 

 

   Yourbody gym - owned by two of my very good
  friends   I left a lot of sweat  in this building! 
    

 

  IKEA - Only the best store ever.  I sure miss it!

 
Other things I miss include:  Summit Nails, Eclipse Sunless Tanning,
and Sandy's natural foods.
