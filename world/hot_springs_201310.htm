Hot Springs, Arkansas

October 13 - 17, 2013

I spent most of a week in Hot Springs for training in my new job (which is great, BTW!)  Just as I was about to leave OKC for the six-hour drive, I got a migraine.  I almost never get them anymore, but I imagine it was the stress of changing jobs that overcame me.  Anyway, I took a bunch of medicine, asked people to pray for me, strapped ice on my forehead, and hit the road.  

The medicine kicked in after about 2 hours so I was starting to feel a little better by the time I reached the Bloomer Bunch in Gore.  I was able to enjoy watching Brodie run wild for about two hours, then it was time to finish the drive to Hot Springs.  

Hot Springs is a nice little town.  My new company, TLI, is a very nice place too.  Friendly people, interesting work, and I get to work out of my house - perfect.  I was pretty focused on learning while I was there, but on my last evening I did some tourist-ing and played with my camera.  It is a very scenic area.
