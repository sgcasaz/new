Friday, February 12, 2010

We took our Chinese friend Iris and our Korean friend Ellie to the Professional Bull Rider's big event in OKC in February.  Our friends the Callantines joined us to complete the group.  It was a great time - mostly because the girls took such delight in all they saw.  It was fun seeing everything through their eyes.  (And catching up with the Callantines too!) 
 
   

 

 The girls were excited to get their picture taken with a real cowboy and this guy was more than happy to oblige them.

 
   
 Iris & Ellie - ready to catch the show  Here we are with the Callantines just before the action started.
   
The girls were captivated from the first explosive, flaming 
opener to the last bull.  It was a great night!
