Shawnee Cleanup with Samaritan's Purse

Monday, May 27, 2013

Although my church was doing another work day in Moore, I decided to go to Shawnee on Memorial Day since I'd heard they were having trouble getting enough volunteers there.  Turns out, volunteers were turned away from Moore in droves on Sunday because of the Presidential visit, so they'd had a lot of help the day before.  But the cleanup work in either town won't be done for months and months, there's plenty to go around! 

You may have heard of Samaritan's Purse as the Operation Christmas Child folks, but they are also big into disaster relief.  I was honored to join their team for the day.  
 
   
The Samaritan's Purse Disaster Response 'office on wheels'.
They had big efforts going in both Moore and Shawnee. 

 

 There were about 4 Samaritan's Purse crews working in this neighborhood, along with at least one other unrelated church crew.  These homes were completely destroyed.
