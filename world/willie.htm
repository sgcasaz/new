willie Nelson's Family Picnic

Sunday, August 4 2002

Megan, Tammy, and I went to a great all-day music festival in the Woodlands.  Bob Schneider, Los Lobos, Jerry Jeff Walker, Lee Ann Womack, and willie all played.  It rained most of the time and was quite muddy - and tons of fun!
