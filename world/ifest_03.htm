International Fest '03

April 27, 2003

The International Festival is my favorite festival in Houston.  This year I decided to become a part of it by volunteering.  I worked beverage booth number 60.  It turned out to be more difficult than you would imagine because the coke was very foamy.  I survived and went on to have a great day when my friends (and husband) joined me for the Charlie Robison concert.  I got up on the front row and had a blast!
