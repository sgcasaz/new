Memorial Weekend 2002

A gang of us got together for a little trip to the Hill Country for Memorial Day.  We started the weekend with a concert - Robert Earl Keen's Texas Uprising.  Nine great acts in an outdoor amphitheater in Selma, TX - not the least of which was my beloved Cowboy Mouth.

The next day we did a float trip down the Guadalupe River on the busiest float day of the year.  It was crazy, but we had a blast!  We spent the night in Austin and headed home the following day.  It was a very good way to spend a long weekend.
