The Bull Thing

August 2017

Eureka is the smallest town in the USA to host this level of PBR event.  It happens each year during the fair and is a big deal for our little town.  It is always one of my favorite events of the year.  This year we had a large contingent of Casazzas there - Grandpa Dan, Dallas, Ashley, Cody, Lily, Hunter, Lane, Aubrey, Dan, Tracie, Larry, Mary Beth, Rebecca, Matt, Katie, and me.  Jeb's family and my husband had also planned to go, but took a detour to the rock climbing area instead.  They missed a great show!
