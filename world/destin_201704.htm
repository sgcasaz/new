Destin - 23rd Anniversary Trip

April 2017

April 9th was our 23rd wedding anniversary so we decided to take a trip to celebrate this year.  We had good memories of Destin after a trip there many years ago for our friend Amy's wedding.  We decided to return to the same resort we visited then so we could enjoy the sugar-sand beaches again.
