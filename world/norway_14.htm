Grete Breivik, my boss while in Norway, very kindly invited me to dinner at her house this evening. They live on an island called Ormøy, just north of Stavanger. The whole island area was very beautiful and quiet. It was only 10 minutes from the city but felt far away.

The Breivik family
 		 Their house, it was located in a beautiful spot with the sea on two sides.  The home was very open and you could see out to the sea from all the living areas.  Very beautiful!
