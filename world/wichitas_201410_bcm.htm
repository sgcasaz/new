Wichita Mountains with International Students

October 18, 2014

The BCM (Baptist Collegiate Ministries) and All Nations Church put on a group trip to the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge in October for International students.  Eric and I joined them to help out where we could, and just to have fun with these great kids.  It was a nice trip.
