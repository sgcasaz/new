November

2020


 
November was a month of unpacking and organizing for me.  I had a 20 foot U-Haul worth of stuff that I had to unload into a home already full of our belongings.  It has been a long process!  But we made time for some fun and adventures along the way. 
 
   

I had to mark the occasion when we let go of two pair of pants Eric had owned for 35 years.
He had made me patch them so many times, there was nothing left to sew into for further patching. 
  A beautiful snowstorm came through one evening.  We built a fire and watched it snow
from the living room with brother Mark and his son Colt.  
   
 We had snow for a week, then it melted again.  Sister-in-law Amy had a friend who was fighting in a cage fighting tournament in Kalispell, 
so we joined her group to watch the event.  It was an experience.  My Mom was jealous!

Then I walked another 30 minutes looking for my @%$# dog who disappeared right at the end of the walk.

We got home and I immediately decorated the tree.   I also put out what few decorations survived my purging of belongings we'd just had in OKC.  
   

The next day I was outside hanging the outdoor lights for several hours.  I came in to
find this devastation.  Gallons of mucky water on the carpet, about a dozen shattered
ornaments, the tree totally undecorated.  It was awful.  Thankfully that lamp survived.

Eric and Mark came in shortly after this and saved the day.  They put the tree back on
it's stand and this time we secured it to the wall.  Then Eric did 80% of the redecorating
because I couldn't face doing it twice in the same 24-hour period.
