Party at PJ & Heather's

Saturday, June 29

Amy had organized a big group trip for us this weekend.  Over 20 of us were supposed to go to the Hill Country for a float down the river, then camping, and a day of rock climbing.  However, the weather had other plans for us.  It rained all weekend and ended up flooding in the San Antonio/Austin area.  Since those plans were dashed, PJ & Heather threw a "too bad we're not on the river" party. They fed us well (as always) and we all had a nice time.
