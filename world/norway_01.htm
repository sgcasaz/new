My little "bean" car - it has been great to have. Check out the key to the car - weird! My hotel room for the first 5 nights.
 
The outlets look different and carry 230 volts. The light switches are all big, flat buttons. The toilets flush similar to those on airplanes.  There are always either knobs which you pull upward or buttons to press to make them flush.
 
This is my apartment building.  It is between Bergelandsgata and Kongsgata streets, near Erichstrups Gate street.  The higher floors overlook the small, beautiful lake called Breiavanet that's in the center of town.  It's a beautiful apartment.  Pictures of the inside are below.

This is the office where I work, it belongs to Petter Hermansen.  He was away in Thiland for a month and didn't mind leaving me his office at all!
