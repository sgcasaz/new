June in Montana

June 2015

I can't seem to stop taking pictures up here, so check them out if you have some time to kill.  
 
   

Lovely view of the field and pond behind Dan & Tracie's house.  This will be the

 An absolutely huge Ponderosa Pine with Eric for scale.  A moose chased him

 Sunset on one of our drives from Kalispell.  You have to be up pretty late to

 My view from my home office - what a huge blessing!
