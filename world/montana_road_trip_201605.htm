Who's ready to go to Montana?   We are!

May 6 - 8, 2016

As the temperature rises, so does our interest in heading north.  We hit the road on our 3-day drive on Friday, May 6th this year.  The cats sure hate the drive, but they LOVE the Montana house - so I guess it all evens out.  
 
   
In the weeks before the trip, they lost more and more real estate on the spare bed. Lily is Eric's co-pilot
