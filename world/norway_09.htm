My Month in Norway - Day 21

Tuesday, July 3

Eirik took me to a lake in Sandness called Lutsi for a beautiful kayak trip. It was so great - very quiet and peaceful - and good exercise too! We paddled for two hours with a snack break in the middle, then headed to Sandness to a very nice restaurant by the water. The whole thing was really a wonderful experience.I arrived home at 10 pm, but since it was still daylight I decided to take a walk downtown. The opening ceremony for the Phillips Open Sand Volleyball tournament had taken place from 7-9, so there were lots of people in town. And I mean lots! I would guess there was maybe 800 people all sitting outside at the bars and restaurants along the harbor. At 10:00 on a Tuesday night! This is just amazing, especially to someone like me who has spent many years in Bartlesville.I was lucky enough to run into a group of people on the waterfront that I know. It was the Phillips PR group who are doing much of the work in putting on the volleyball tournament. Their names are: Ingvar Solberg, Kurt Michelsen, and Elisabeth Huse. They invited me to join them and we had so much fun! We had a really nice visit and then went out for an Irish Coffee and visited some more. I finally got home at 2 am! It made me sad that I was not getting the day off for the 4th of July Holiday since I really needed the extra sleep! Here are some pictures that Eirik very kindly took of me on the kayak trip - notice I'm smiling in every shot, it was a lot of fun!
