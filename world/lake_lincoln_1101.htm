Climbing at Lake Lincoln

Thanksgiving Day 2001

 
Eric never misses a chance to go climbing.  This Thanksgiving was a big climbing weekend for him.  His climbing buddies Pete and Elizabeth drove up from Houston to Oklahoma to spend the holiday climbing with Eric.  They climbed every day (with a break for Thanksgiving dinner, of course!) and had a great time.  Here are some shots of them at Lake Lincoln in Arkansas.
 
These shots are just to show how pretty it is over there.  This place is only about 12 miles from my hometown, but I never knew it existed until Eric started rock climbing.  It's a really nice area.
   
Here's the gang at the rock.  Pete is about to start a climb. Elizabeth belaying Eric while he makes yet another climb look easy.
   

Me and Mom watching and being impressed.
