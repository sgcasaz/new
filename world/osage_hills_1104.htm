Osage Hills Weekend Getaway

November 12 - 14, 2004

When we lived in Bartlesville, we would always stay in the cabins at Osage Hills a couple of times a year.  We decided it would be nice to do that again, now that we are living back in Oklahoma.  On our second night there we invited some of our old friends out for a cookout.  It was very cold but a few true friends braved the weather to come see us.  It was fun catching up with everyone.  It was a great weekend, I would recommend the cabins at Osage Hills State Park to anyone.
